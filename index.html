Value Bets: Football & Ice Hockey

Status (v4, Oct 2026): The model-based rules did not pass a strict rolling walk-forward test and are no longer bets (shown as unstaked "model lean"). Adopted: price-based football rules that beat the market-average no-vig price: F11 best named soft book ≥8% above it (odds ≤4), F2 max price across books ≥10% above it. Out-of-sample 2014-27: 678 bets, ROI +16.3% (95% CI +1.5% to +32.4%), CLV +10.5% (CI +9.4% to +11.7%), CLV positive in 13/13 seasons. But most of that edge came from soft books that no longer quote (Interwetten) and from 2014-22; 2022-27 has only about 50 bets. Expect very few picks, low limits and the risk of restricted accounts. Picks only appear for fixtures in the football-data.co.uk odds file (updated Tue/Fri). Details in the Backtest tab.

Value at ≥ = minimum odds at which a selection would clear the EV threshold on the pure model (use it when we have no odds feed, e.g. Swiss leagues). Elo = strength rating; form = average result vs Elo expectation over the last 6 games (opponent-strength-adjusted streak); H2H = same, over recent head-to-head meetings.
